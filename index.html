About us

Pizzaria is a small family kitchen with one simple belief: great pizza doesn't need to be complicated. We make our dough fresh every morning, top every pie with generous servings of your favorite flavors, and bake it in a wood-fired oven until the edges are crisp. That's it. That's the whole philosophy.

  • Baked fresh — Dough made every morning, never frozen.
  • Wood-fired — 900°F oven, 90-second bake. Crispy edges, soft center.
  • Made for sharing — Sizes good for one, four, or the whole group.
